# In this town… (≈45 s, fiction)

**Hook:** "In this town, whatever you pretend to be for 30 days… becomes real."

## Why this should perform
- **It uses the proven "In this town…" template** from your reference: an absurd rule, then escalating payoffs ($0 → $3 → +$1,000,000).
- **It ties into your series theme:** "larp until you get it", turned literally into a superpower.
- **The twist ending is built for comments.** The best friend was pretending to be *him*, so viewers argue about who keeps the money. Open-loop and twist hooks are what hook-testing write-ups rank highest ([Hook Mafia](https://www.hookmafia.io/blog/i-tested-30-viral-tiktok-hooks-here-s-what-actually-works-in-2026), [Sozee](https://www.sozee.ai/resources/tiktok-trends-visual-stories/)). These are marketing blogs, not official TikTok data.
- **It ends with "Part 2?"**, which gives people a reason to follow.
