# The Great Impostor: Part 1 (≈60 s)

## Why this story
- **It fits the series.** "Larp until you get it", taken to the extreme: a man who faked being a surgeon, and his patients survived.
- **The hook is a contradiction:** "He had zero medical training. So he became a navy surgeon." Contrarian and open-loop hooks are among the openers that marketing write-ups test best ([Hook Mafia](https://www.hookmafia.io/blog/i-tested-30-viral-tiktok-hooks-here-s-what-actually-works-in-2026), [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/viral-hooks-library)). Those are vendor blogs, not TikTok data.
- **It follows hook → build → payoff.** That is the three-part structure recommended for visual story series ([Sozee](https://www.sozee.ai/resources/tiktok-trends-visual-stories/)).
- **It ends on an open loop** ("Part 2: his other fake lives"), which sets up the next episode.

## Voiceover script (read over the captions)
> He had zero medical training. So he became a navy surgeon.
> 1951. The Korean War. Canadian destroyer HMCS Cayuga.
> His name: Ferdinand Waldo Demara. He befriended a real doctor, Joseph Cyr… and used his credentials.

> Then wounded men were brought aboard. One had a bullet near his heart.

> The navy was embarrassed. No charges. They just sent him back to the US.
> Hollywood turned it into a movie: The Great Impostor, 1961.
> Larp until you get it… but maybe not as a surgeon. Part 2: his other fake lives.
